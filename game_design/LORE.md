# Lore — the world, the voice, and the truth

Transcribed by hand from `../../infinite-delve/game_design/WORLD.md` and `LORE.md`,
then **re-banded to the v5 strata** and extended to the full roster. The fiction is
the one irreplaceable creative asset from that project, and the mockup already speaks
it: warrens, crypt, abyss, the lantern, *"THE THING AT SIXTY."*

Nothing here is imported as code. It is prose, and it stays prose.

**Fragment-first.** Players never see a lore wall — only pieces, surfacing through
enemy names, item flavour, descent lines, and season posts. The comment section is
the intended assembly point. Lore never gates gameplay; skipping every fragment costs
nothing but wonder.

---

## Tone

**Lantern-lit grit.** The world is dark and the work is dangerous; the people doing it
are weathered, wry, and proud. Humour exists but it's miners' humour — dry,
understated, earned. Never slapstick, never cute. Stakes are played straight: the
Delve takes people's hauls and sometimes their nerve, and they go back down anyway.
**That defiance is the brand.**

Think veteran foreman reading the day's report by lamplight. Not doom metal, not a
storybook. Violence is dark-fantasy stylised — flashes and shadows, never gore
(all-ages Reddit surface).

## The fiction — one paragraph, canonical

> Above the shaft there is a camp, and a lantern, and not much else. Below it the
> ground opened into **the Delve** — a bottomless, shifting deep that rearranges
> itself every night and pays better the further down you go. Everyone in the
> subreddit descends the same shaft each morning, because by morning it is the same
> shaft *for everyone* and a different one from yesterday. Your lantern decides how
> far ahead you can read. Dying costs you what you were carrying; it never costs you
> the way back up.

Canon anchors: **the camp** (the hub, the only warm screen), **the lantern**
(foresight, and the cosmetic you see in every frame), **the sub is the crew**, *"the
Delve rearranges nightly"* = the daily seed **and the seeded cast**, **the community
shaft** = everyone's depths pooled into one dig.

## ⚠️ THE TRUTH — for authors, almost never shown

**It digs back.** The Delve is not a place. It is the *works* of something enormous
that is digging upward, and has been for longer than the camp has existed. Every
"depth" is a finished part of its excavation — a chamber it has already passed
through. The deeper you go, the closer you are to where it is currently working.

Every fragment must obey all five rules:

1. **The nightly rearrangement is movement.** The Delve changes daily because It
   progressed in the night. Maps are wrong by morning; **the daily seed IS this
   fact**, and so is the fact that the cast at depth 7 is not the cast that was there
   yesterday.
2. **The deep things are heralds, not invaders.** They surface *ahead* of the work,
   the way dust precedes a drill. They do not want the camp; they are arriving before
   something that doesn't want anything we understand.
3. **Loot is payment — or tailings.** Gear and shards are what the work discards (or
   leaves deliberately; never confirmed which). The crew's joke *"the Delve pays
   well"* is more true than anyone likes.
4. **You can linger where It has been, never where It is.** That is why surfacing
   exists, why the shaft gets harder as It is approached, and why the monsters are
   wrong-shaped: an ecosystem displaced upward, fleeing or following. **The wanderers
   are this rule made mechanical** — things that belong to no stratum, met anywhere.
5. **It is never named, never seen, never confirmed.** Fragments refer only to: *the
   work · the sound · the warmth · below.* Player-invented names become community
   canon; we never correct them.

**Never confirmed, ever** — the questions *are* the content: what It is · why It digs ·
whether It knows about us · whether It can be stopped · how deep It started · what
happens when It arrives.

**The Thing at Sixty** (screen 16, deferred) and **The Listener** (the secret boss)
are the only two places the fiction points directly at this, and it is why both are
worth deferring rather than cutting.

## Delivery rules

- **Fragments only.** No cutscenes, no quest text, no codex essays.
- **Word caps:** item flavour ≤20 · descent line ≤25 · enemy line ≤25 · boss intro ≤30.
- **Three tiers:** 🕯️ *flavour* (atmosphere, safe anywhere) · 🧩 *pattern* (a canon rule
  glimpsed — meaningful on reread) · 🔦 *revelation* (near-truth; RARE — the floor,
  deep Endless, community finales).
- Every fragment is written in the foreman's register and truth-checked against the
  five rules before it ships.

## Naming conventions

| Type | Pattern | Canon examples |
|---|---|---|
| Strata | evocative place, 1–2 words, **≤7 chars for the grid label** | the Warrens, the Hold, the Crypt, the Abyss |
| Enemies | plain Adjective+Noun, readable at a glance | Ratling, Cave Hound, Goblin Shaman, Pale Forager |
| Bosses | "The X" or Name+Title | The Hollow King, Goblin Chieftain, Broodmother |
| Abilities | verb-noun or two punchy words | Strike, Iron Will, Piercing Shot, Whirlwind |
| Ultimates | one heavy word, or two | Execute, Pyroclasm, Last Stand, Reckoning |
| Boons | two words, states the trade | Twin Edge, Standing Guard, Second Lung |
| Gear — procedural | **`{Rarity} {Base}`, derived, never authored** | Uncommon Pick, Rare Coat, Common Knot |
| Gear — uniques | two evocative words, no "of the" chains | Gutripper, Everburn, Null Sigil, Aegis Heart |
| Gear — sets | possessive or compound + noun | Warden's Vigil, Cindersworn, Voidbound |
| Classes | one plain role noun | Warden · Hunter · Adept |
| Specialisations | one heavier noun, ≤11 chars for the pixel font | Bulwark · Reaver · Ranger · Bloodhound · Pyromancer · Voidcaller |
| Elements | one plain word | Fire · Frost · Shock · Void |
| Cosmetics | one evocative word | Amber, Voidlight, Cracked Coin |
| Delvers | compound nature-words, no fantasy apostrophes | quartzfen, mossgrave, sable_arc, tarnwick, lantern_ox, Vesk |

Rules: **no apostrophe-heavy fantasy gibberish** (no "Zar'keth"). Names must read at
pixel-font sizes — the UI sets them in 7–13px Silkscreen, so anything over ~14
characters truncates, and stratum labels are tighter than that. Every proper noun gets
**one** canonical spelling, recorded here.

**Procedural gear names are generated, not written.** `{Rarity} {Base}` from
[GEAR.md](GEAR.md)'s twelve bases. Only uniques and sets are hand-named — which is
what makes finding one feel different from finding the ninth Uncommon Coat.

## Reddit post voice

Written as **ledger entries from the camp's foreman** — terse, weathered, proud of the
crew, names real players. Short sentences. One emoji max per title.

- **Daily post:** `⛏️ DELVE 128 — how deep did you get?` Body carries the count, the
  average, and how many reached the floor. Yesterday's grid shape sits on the card.
- **Someone hits the floor:** "Three of you touched the bottom of 128. The ledger
  remembers. Tomorrow the shaft is somewhere else."
- **Community shaft milestone:** "We're at forty-three metres. The crew has dug worse
  in worse weeks."

**Never** corporate, never system-speak ("engagement", "rewards program"), never shame
a shallow run, never break the weathered-pride register for a meme.
